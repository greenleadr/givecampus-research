Role Context

Senior Product Manager

Reports to the Head of Product. Works cross-functionally with Engineering, Data Science, Partner Operations, Partner Success, Marketing, and Business Development.


What the job posting signals:

  • 5+ years PM experience required; startup experience preferred
  • Comfort with CRM concepts
  • Both B2B and B2C sensibility
  • Emphasis on "driving user trial/adoption/usage" — this is a growth-stage PM role, not a maintenance role
  • Target: 100% year-over-year growth in philanthropic dollars processed
Culture Signal (Glassdoor: 3.2/5)

What's praised

  • People described as kind, bright, mission-driven
  • Meaningful work in education

What's flagged

  • Leadership turnover & shifting priorities
  • Founders reluctant to cede control
  • Below-average pay for non-management roles
  • Retention issues
Tip: Multiple Glassdoor reviewers recommend specifically asking junior and mid-level staff how culture has evolved.

Macro Tailwinds & Headwinds

Headwinds (but why GC's value prop gets more urgent)

  • Projected 13% enrollment decline through 2041
  • International student enrollment down 17% in fall 2025
  • More than half of private universities running operating deficits
  • Grad PLUS loan elimination starting July 2026 will further depress institutional revenue
  • Donor base consolidating — total giving up but individual donor count declining

Tailwinds

  • Schools need fundraising to work harder because tuition revenue is shrinking — urgency for GC's value prop
  • 55% of K-12 schools and 51% of higher ed institutions reported fundraising growth last year
  • $100 trillion intergenerational wealth transfer from Boomers reshaping donor landscape
Competitive Landscape

Blackbaud

The 800-pound gorilla. Broad nonprofit focus, legacy infrastructure, slower on AI and modern UX. Currently lists GiveCampus as a partner — not yet perceived as a direct enterprise threat, but that could change.

Primary Incumbent

EverTrue

Competes specifically on donor intelligence and social data for advancement teams. More direct overlap with GC Intelligence and GC Gift Officer.

Intelligence Competitor
GiveCampus differentiation: Education-only focus + faster AI execution + modern UX. The Blackbaud partnership is strategically interesting but fragile — if Blackbaud decides to build or acquire rather than partner, dynamics shift.
AI Strategy

GC Intelligence — Central Differentiation Bet

Being woven into every part of the platform. The key question to ask: are they measuring AI impact on actual fundraising outcomes? Features are easy to ship and hard to prove value for.
